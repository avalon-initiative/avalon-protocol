# Using the Hub

This page is for you if you're a player or user of a game, app, or service
that connects to Avalon — not a developer, not someone hosting a server.
No jargon assumed; if a word here doesn't make sense, check the
[glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## What the Hub is for

Every game or app you use can show you *its own* version of your Avalon
identity — your friends, your guild, your achievements — inside itself. The
Hub is the same information, but on its own, outside of any one game. Open
it any time you want to check your friends list, see what you've achieved
across everything you play, or manage settings that apply to your identity
as a whole rather than to one game specifically.

You don't need an account with "the Hub" separately — it's the same Avalon
identity you use everywhere else.

## Logging in

Avalon identities don't use passwords. You log in with a passkey — the same
kind of thing your phone or browser already uses for Face ID, a fingerprint,
or a PIN. The first time, you create an identity and register a passkey for
it; after that, logging in is just confirming with that passkey again, on
that device or a new one you've paired.

- **Lost every device with a registered passkey?** Avalon supports social
  recovery — a small group of people you trust (guardians) can help you get
  back in, without anyone holding a master key to your identity. Set this
  up under your profile before you need it.
- **Adding a new phone or computer?** You can pair a new device to your
  existing identity rather than starting over — it stays the same identity,
  same friends, same history.

## What you can do here

- **Profile** — a short self-description, the passkeys and devices
  registered to your identity, and your guardian recovery setup.
- **Friends** — see and manage the friends list that follows you between
  every game and app you use, not just one of them.
- **Guilds** — your guild memberships, guild chat, and guild events, as a
  network-level thing rather than something owned by any one game.
- **Achievements** — everything issued to you across every game/app/service
  you've connected, each one showing who issued it, so you always know
  where it came from.
- **Messages** — conversations with friends, independent of which game
  either of you currently has open.
- **Connections / integration directory** — see which games, apps, and
  services you've connected your identity to, what each one can and can't
  see about you, and disconnect one if you change your mind.

## What the Hub is not

The Hub doesn't run any game itself, doesn't store your in-game character,
inventory, or progress, and can't see anything inside a game or app beyond
what that game or app has chosen to share with Avalon. Losing access to a
game doesn't cost you your Avalon identity, friends, or achievement
history — that's the whole point.
